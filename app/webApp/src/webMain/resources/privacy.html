ACT — Privacy policy

ACT is an unofficial client for your MyAnimeList (MAL) anime list, for Android, desktop and the web. This page says what it keeps, where, and who it talks to.

Summary

  • You sign in on myanimelist.net. ACT never sees your MAL password.
  • ACT has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and shares nothing with anyone.
  • The only copy of your data that ACT keeps is a sign-in token on your own device.

What is stored on your device

After you sign in, ACT stores the access and refresh tokens MAL issued, your MAL user name, the MAL Client ID the app signed in with, and your chosen list layout. It stores nothing else about you.

  • Android: in the app's private storage. The app opts out of cloud and device-to-device backup, so the tokens are not copied off the device.
  • Desktop: in a file readable only by your user account, in your state directory ($XDG_STATE_HOME, usually ~/.local/state, on Linux).
  • Web: in your browser's session storage for this site. It is discarded when you close the tab.

Signing out deletes the tokens. Uninstalling the app, or deleting its data, removes everything it stored.

What is sent to MyAnimeList

To show and update your list, ACT sends requests to MAL (myanimelist.net and api.myanimelist.net) carrying your access token: searching, loading your list and anime details, and saving changes you make to a list entry. Cover images are loaded from MAL's image servers. MAL handles that data under its own privacy policy.

The web relay

MAL does not let browsers call its API directly, so the web version sends those requests through a small relay server at act.io-workshop.net. The relay forwards each request to MAL and returns MAL's answer. It does not store, and does not log, tokens, authorization codes or request and response bodies, and has no database.

To limit abuse, the relay counts requests per IP address. Those counters are held in memory only, are never written to disk, and are discarded when the server restarts. The server in front of the relay may keep ordinary connection logs (such as IP address and time) for operating the service. The Android and desktop apps do not use the relay.

What ACT does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No sharing or sale of data. There is no third party to share it with.
  • No account with ACT, so there is no ACT account to delete.

Deleting your data

Sign out in the app, or clear its data, to remove what is on your device. To cut ACT's access to your MAL account, revoke it under Account Settings → Apps on myanimelist.net. Your list itself lives at MAL and is deleted or changed through your MAL account.

Age

ACT is intended for people aged 18 and over, because MAL lists can include mature imagery.
