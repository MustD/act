ACT — Anime Control Terminal

ACT is a client for browsing and updating your MyAnimeList (MAL) anime list, for Android, desktop and the web.

A MyAnimeList account is required

ACT works on your own MAL list, so you need a MyAnimeList account. You sign in on myanimelist.net; ACT never sees your MAL password. You can make an account at myanimelist.net.

Unofficial

ACT is not affiliated with or endorsed by MyAnimeList. Anime data is provided by MyAnimeList.net.
